Homemade Lasagna

A delicious homemade lasagna with tender beef, creamy white sauce and cheese.

Lasagna Gallery

Ingredients

  • 1 kg minced meat
  • 500 g lasagna sheets
  • 2 cups cheese
  • 1 cup white sauce
  • 3 tomatoes

Spices

  • Salt
  • Cumin
  • Red chilli
  • Black pepper
  • Paprika

Cooking Instructions

  1. Boil the lasagna sheets.
  2. Chop the required ingredients.
  3. Cook the minced beef for approximately 20 minutes.
  4. Arrange the sheets, beef, white sauce and cheese in layers.
  5. Bake for approximately 35 minutes.
